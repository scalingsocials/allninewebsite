Overview

Financial clarity your leadership and investors can rely on.

Strong finance gives leadership a clear view of performance and gives investors a reason to trust the numbers.

We act as your CFO office, from planning and monthly reporting to valuations and raising capital. Whether you are tightening up your numbers, preparing for investors or putting a value on your business, we bring the kind of financial expertise usually reserved for large companies.

Through tailored models and reporting, leadership teams can anticipate challenges, allocate capital well and pursue opportunities with confidence.

FAQs

Questions we often hear

How can we engage you?

Three ways: project-based (financial models, valuations, pitch decks), fractional or hourly (budget reviews, variance analysis, capital allocation guidance), or a full partnership with complete FP&A outsourcing and investor reporting.

Who leads the work?

Engagements are led by a team of qualified professionals with more than two decades of experience in finance and advisory. See our team.

Will you work with our existing accountants?

Yes. We usually work alongside your accounting team or external accountants and add the planning, analysis and strategic layer on top.

Do you provide valuations for regulatory purposes?

We provide business, share and intangible asset valuations and fairness opinions. Tell us the purpose of the valuation and we will confirm the approach and any registration requirements.
