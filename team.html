Mussarat Jahan

Director

Specialist, Transaction Advisory, Strategy & Finance

mussarat.jahan@allninecpl.com
“Helping businesses grow smarter, scale stronger, and create lasting value.”

Mussarat Jahan is a seasoned professional, a Chartered Accountant and CFA (Charter pending). She brings over two decades of experience advising businesses on strategic growth, financial transformation, capital allocation, valuation and transaction execution. Having led complex finance and advisory engagements across global organizations, she combines strategic insight with deep financial expertise to help businesses achieve sustainable growth and maximize stakeholder value.

Her experience spans transaction advisory, mergers and acquisitions, business valuation, financial planning and analysis, capital raising and corporate finance. She has supported founders, growth-stage companies and established enterprises through critical decisions on expansion, investments, restructuring, funding and performance improvement.

Before her independent advisory practice, she led strategic finance and FP&A initiatives at RSM US Integrated Services India, and spent over thirteen years with KPMG Global Services, where she managed financial modelling, valuation, deal advisory and corporate finance engagements across diverse industries. She also built and led large multidisciplinary teams delivering institutional-grade financial solutions and transaction support.

Her advisory philosophy is rooted in creating measurable business value through strategic thinking, financial rigour and execution-focused solutions. She works closely with business owners, leadership teams, investors and stakeholders to improve profitability, strengthen governance, unlock growth opportunities and enhance enterprise value.
