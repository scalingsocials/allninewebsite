Transaction advisory, built for MSMEs

Capital raising, M&A advisory, valuations and deal readiness for founder-led MSMEs with deals between ₹5 Cr and ₹250 Cr.

MSMEs drive around 30% of India’s GDP and 45% of its exports, yet they sit in a gap most investment banks don’t serve. Tickets are too small for bulge-bracket banks, yet too complex for unstructured execution. We fill that gap with institutional-grade modelling, valuations, investor materials and deal management.

Equity capital raising

Angel, PE and VC fundraising: investor targeting, pitch decks, financial models and term sheet support.

M&A advisory

Sell-side and buy-side execution, including succession-driven sales, strategic acquisitions, target screening and negotiation through close.

Valuations

DCF, comparable and transaction-multiple valuations; pre-money / post-money analysis, fairness opinions and ESOP valuations.

Deal readiness

Books clean-up, financial diligence prep, data room build-out and investor narrative, so you are deal-ready before going to market.

Transaction advisory

Independent business case review, sensitivity analysis, financial diligence and deal structuring across the transaction lifecycle.

Strategic advisory

Business plan refinement, unit economics, capital structure design and growth strategy, so the case is built before the raise.
