Overview

Strategy that is tested against the numbers.

Good strategy answers three questions: where to grow, how to fund it and what it will return.

We work with founders, promoters and leadership teams to set direction and turn it into a plan the business can execute. That can mean entering a new market, reshaping the business model, turning around a unit that is losing money, or building long-term value for the promoter group.

Because our strategy and finance teams work together, every recommendation comes with the financial case behind it.

How we work

From a clear plan to results you can measure.

Strategy only matters once it shows up in the numbers. Each engagement moves through four stages, with agreed milestones at each one.

Assess

Where the business stands today: markets, margins, capital and capability, and the gap to where you want to be.

Design

A roadmap with priorities, owners, investment needs and the financial case for each move.

Execute

Hands-on support through implementation, from restructuring and market entry to cost and pricing programs.

Measure

Regular tracking against the plan, so leadership sees what is working and adjusts early.

FAQs

Questions we often hear

How is strategy advisory different from consulting advisory?

Strategy advisory sets direction: where to grow, how to restructure and how to build value. Consulting advisory supports transactions, due diligence, governance and controls. Many clients use both.

Do you help with implementation?

Yes. We stay involved through execution, with milestones and regular tracking against the plan.

Can you support a turnaround?

Yes. We start with a business and cash diagnostic, then build a turnaround plan and support conversations with lenders and other stakeholders.

How are engagements priced?

Strategy work is usually project-based with a fixed fee. Ongoing implementation support can be priced as a retainer.
