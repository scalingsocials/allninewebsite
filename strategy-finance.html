Overview

Financial expertise usually reserved for large companies.

We help organisations achieve sustainable growth by aligning financial strategy with corporate objectives and enabling data-driven decisions.

Whether you’re launching something new, planning a big investment, tightening up your numbers, buying or selling a business, or getting ready to step away, we bring the kind of financial expertise usually reserved for large companies.

Through bespoke analysis and tailored financial models, we give leadership teams the insight to anticipate challenges, allocate capital effectively and pursue opportunities with confidence.

FAQs

Questions we often hear

How can we engage you?

Three ways: project-based (financial models, valuation support, pitch decks), fractional or hourly (budget reviews, variance analysis, capital allocation guidance), or a full partnership with complete FP&A outsourcing and investor reporting.

Who leads the work?

Engagements are led by a Chartered Accountant and CFA (All levels) with more than two decades in professional services, including Big 4 experience.

Will you work with our existing accountants?

Yes. We usually work alongside your accounting team or external accountants, adding the planning, analysis and strategic layer on top.

Do you support PE funds and family offices?

Yes. We build fund cash flow, performance and regulatory reporting models, and run FP&A and consolidated investor reporting. See Who we serve.
