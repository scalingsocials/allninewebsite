FAQs

Questions we often hear

What is a virtual CFO?

A virtual CFO is an experienced finance leader who works with you part-time — handling reporting, forecasting, cash management and strategic finance — at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.

Will you work with our existing accountants?

Yes. We usually work alongside your accounting team or external accountants, adding the planning, analysis and leadership layer on top.

Can you help us prepare for a fundraise?

Yes — we build the financial model, prepare the data room, support valuation and help you answer investor questions with confidence.

How long are engagements?

Projects like valuations or models typically run a few weeks. Virtual CFO engagements are ongoing, usually on a monthly retainer.
