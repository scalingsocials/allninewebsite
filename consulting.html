Overview

Compliance handled. Risk understood. Growth unblocked.

Regulation keeps changing. Your business shouldn't have to slow down because of it.

Our consulting practice keeps companies compliant, well-governed and ready for growth. We handle the statutory and regulatory work end-to-end, give your board independent assurance on risk and controls, and advise on the structural decisions that shape your business — from setting up in India to entering a new market or bringing in investors.

You get one accountable team, a clear compliance calendar and advice you can actually act on.

Our Consulting services

Pick a single service or combine them into an ongoing engagement — scoped around your business, not a template.

Corporate & Secretarial Compliance

End-to-end compliance under the Companies Act and LLP Act, so your board can focus on the business.

  • Incorporation of companies, LLPs and branch offices
  • Annual ROC filings, board & AGM support
  • Statutory registers, minutes and resolutions
  • Changes in directors, capital and registered office

Regulatory, FEMA & Licensing

Navigate the regulators with confidence — from foreign investment reporting to sector licences.

  • FDI and ODI structuring and reporting
  • RBI and FEMA filings and compounding
  • Business registrations and licences
  • Compliance health-checks and remediation

Internal Audit & Risk Advisory

Independent reviews that strengthen controls and give your board real assurance.

  • Risk-based internal audit plans
  • Internal financial controls (IFC) reviews
  • Process and SOP documentation
  • Fraud risk and vendor reviews

Due Diligence & Business Advisory

Clear-eyed analysis before you invest, acquire, partner or restructure.

  • Financial, tax and compliance due diligence
  • Entity structuring and restructuring
  • Business set-up for foreign companies in India
  • Board and promoter advisory

FAQs

Questions we often hear

Do you handle compliance for companies outside Bangalore?

Yes. Most of our work is delivered digitally, so we support companies across India, and foreign businesses with Indian entities.

Can you take over compliance from our current consultant?

Absolutely. We start with a compliance health-check, close any gaps we find and then take ownership of the calendar going forward.

Do you work with LLPs and startups?

Yes — from newly incorporated startups and LLPs to established private and unlisted public companies.

How are your fees structured?

Depending on scope, we offer fixed fees for defined projects and monthly retainers for ongoing compliance. You'll receive a clear proposal before any work begins.
