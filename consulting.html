FAQs

Questions we often hear

Do you handle compliance for companies outside Bangalore?

Yes. Most of our work is delivered digitally, so we support companies across India, and foreign businesses with Indian entities.

Can you take over compliance from our current consultant?

Absolutely. We start with a compliance health-check, close any gaps we find and then take ownership of the calendar going forward.

Do you work with startups?

Yes. Our CFO services for startups cover every stage — from incorporation and early operations to fundraising and post-funding governance. See the stage-by-stage view on this page.

How are your fees structured?

Depending on scope, we offer fixed fees for defined projects and monthly retainers for ongoing compliance. You'll receive a clear proposal before any work begins.
