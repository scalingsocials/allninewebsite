Overview

Every return on time. Every rupee accounted for.

Good tax work is invisible — returns filed, credits claimed, no surprises.

Our tax practice covers corporate and direct tax, GST and international tax under one roof. We keep you compliant with every filing, find the credits and deductions you're entitled to, and structure transactions so they hold up to scrutiny.

And if a notice does arrive, we handle it with the same care — from the first response to representation.

FAQs

Questions we often hear

Do you handle both GST and income tax?

Yes. We handle direct and indirect tax together, which helps avoid mismatches between GST returns, books and income tax filings.

Can you help with a tax notice we've already received?

Yes. Share the notice with us and we'll review it, explain the exposure and prepare a well-documented response within the timeline given.

Do you advise on cross-border transactions?

Yes — treaty analysis, withholding tax, transfer pricing and permanent establishment questions are a core part of our international tax practice.

Do you work with individuals?

We primarily work with businesses, and also support their founders, promoters, senior management and NRIs with personal tax matters.
