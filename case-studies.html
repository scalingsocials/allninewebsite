Case study

Cash flow visibility & working capital transformation.

Unlocking liquidity and de-risking growth through a 13-week rolling forecast and working capital discipline.

IndustryFood manufacturer
RevenueINR 300 Cr
Engagement8 to 12 weeks
ServiceStrategy & Finance
The challenge

Strong top-line growth was masking real cash strain.

The client was navigating rapid growth alongside tightening liquidity, and leadership lacked the forward visibility needed to plan with confidence.

  • Limited visibility into the near-term cash position, making it hard to plan vendor payments, debt servicing and growth investments
  • Stretched working capital cycles: receivables ageing beyond terms, inventory build-ups in slow-moving SKUs, inconsistent payable discipline
  • Fragmented forecasting across Treasury, FP&A and Operations, in disconnected spreadsheets with inconsistent assumptions
  • Reactive cash decisions, with shortfalls surfacing too late to negotiate favourable terms with lenders or suppliers
Our approach

Three steps from diagnosis to discipline.

Diagnostic & baseline

Reviewed cash reporting, AR/AP and inventory policies and historical patterns to find leakage and forecast gaps. Interviewed Finance, Sales, Procurement and Operations to map the true cash cycle.

13-week cash flow model

Designed a dynamic, driver-based direct-method forecast integrated with long-range planning, with full scenario and sensitivity capability.

Working capital optimisation

Tightened collection protocols on ageing receivables, rationalised inventory through SKU-level analysis, standardised payment terms, and introduced KPIs and a weekly cash review.

Key deliverables

Three integrated tools, handed over with SOPs and training.

13-week rolling cash flow model

Direct-method forecast with weekly variance tracking, a category-level receipts and payments view, and early-warning indicators.

Working capital dashboard

DSO, DPO, DIO and cash conversion cycle, tracked in one place for Finance and Treasury.

Liquidity planning & scenarios

Base, upside and downside scenario modelling to plan capex, debt and supplier decisions ahead of time.

Benefits delivered

Results the board can see.

₹85 lakhWorking capital released
28 daysTaken off the cash conversion cycle
95%Forecast accuracy, up from about 75%
12 daysFaster customer collections (DSO)
  • Forecast accuracy 13-week forecasts now track within ±5% of actuals, up from ±15 to 20%.
  • Reporting efficiency The weekly cash reporting cycle fell from 2 to 3 days to under half a day through automation.
  • Board-ready reporting Leadership receives one reconciled cash view every week, enabling proactive decisions on capex timing, debt paydown and supplier negotiations.
  • Covenant headroom Early liquidity visibility let the client pre-empt covenant tightness and renegotiate with lenders on favourable terms.
  • Team capability Finance and Treasury teams were trained to own, update and evolve the model after handover.
Client impact

What began as a cash visibility problem became a broader finance transformation.

The client now runs its business with a forward-looking cash lens. That means confident investment decisions, stronger lender and supplier relationships, and a finance function that is a true strategic partner to the business.
